How it works

1

Screenshot the invite, poll or message

Any app, any format — WhatsApp, Teamer, Spond, ClubForce, a school text, an email.

2

Open Cluichi → Add → 📷

Tap the camera button and choose the screenshot from your photos.

3

Check the details and save

Child, date, time and location are filled in for you — tweak anything, then tap Save.

Get it installed — takes a minute, once

📱 iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open family-org-omega.vercel.app
  2. Tap the Share icon (box with an arrow) along the bottom bar
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open family-org-omega.vercel.app
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then confirm

🔑 Join the family

  1. Enter your family name + shared passphrase — same two things for everyone
  2. Go to Manage and add each child once
  3. Share that name + passphrase with anyone else who should see the calendar
